How this would work for you

Customers get an instant receipt with their details, so there are no “did you get my email?” calls and no requests lost in an inbox. Every request lands in one list with a status. Staff answer with a quote when ready, same as today.

1

Customer fills form

Name, item, date, and details — the same information you already ask for.

2

Instant confirmation email

They receive a receipt of what they sent. No call to check that it arrived.

3

Bakery reviews & sends quote

Staff still approve the request and reply when ready, as you do now.

What Kellan would set up with real tools

  • Your existing online order forms already run on Jotform — turn on an autoresponder email with the customer’s answers and the next steps (a site form or Google Form can do the same).
  • Zapier or Make adds each request to a Google Sheet and pings staff when a new row appears.
  • Staff still reply from Gmail after they review the request. The automatic note is only the receipt.
  • This sits beside your existing SimplyBook bridal consultation booking, which stays unchanged.

Estimate: a few minutes saved per order that would have needed a check-in call, plus fewer “did you get my email?” interruptions during bakery hours. This is a general estimate, not a measured result.
